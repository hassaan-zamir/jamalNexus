# The Six-Dimension Rubric

Score every piece of work 1–10 on each dimension. Always give the number AND the reason.

1. **Sound design** — narration clarity, music placement, SFX layering, no digital silence, correct loudness (−16 LUFS narration master, −24 LUFS beds).
2. **Rhythm** — cut timing vs music/narration, shot-length variation, pauses that land.
3. **Colour consistency** — one grade across all sources; era treatments correct.
4. **Typography** — two typefaces max, fixed grid, fixed margins, correct hierarchy.
5. **Motion quality** — named easing curves, house durations, motion decelerates into rest, never linear.
6. **Restraint** — few moves executed perfectly; transition whitelist respected.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Distracting errors, visible to any viewer |
| 4–5 | Competent, reads as amateur |
| 6–7 | Professional to a general audience |
| 8–9 | Professional to an editor |
| 10  | Indistinguishable from reference channels |

**Phase gates:** ≥5 avg at end of P1 · ≥6.5 avg at end of P4 · ≥8 avg (no dim <7) at end of P6.

**Belts:** White (setup) → Yellow (audio chain owned) → Orange (LUT pack) → Green (overlay pack) → Blue (music DB) → Purple (zero-question spec) → Brown (QC checklist live) → Black (P6 ships ≥8). Belt advances on demonstrated capability, never time served. Record belt changes in state/progress.json.
